# Wave 1 — official task facts

Verified 2026-10-24 against current Pearson PTE Academic task pages and the Score
Guide linked by Pearson's scoring page. `PTE_SPEC_VERSION` remains `2026-10-01`:
this records verification of that specification, not a new scoring implementation.
No unofficial task weights are encoded.

Summarize Spoken Text Form bands were rechecked against the current Pearson Score Guide on 2026-10-24: 2 points for 50–70 words; 1 point for 40–49 or 71–100; 0 below 40 or above 100. The guide also identifies all-capitals, no punctuation, bullet-only and very-short-sentence responses as zero Form. The practice app approximates these structural checks and does not claim Pearson scoring equivalence. SWT responses shorter than five or longer than 75 words receive zero. The SST
50–70-word target is different: the guide awards partial Form credit at 40–49 or
71–100 words; below 40 or above 100 receives zero. Other form/content rules still
apply. Do not implement a zero-score rule merely for leaving SST's target band.
SGD's typical two-to-three-minute description is not a mandatory two-minute floor.
HIW uses the displayed transcript and one audio playback, with selections marking
words that differ from speech.

Describe Image facts were reverified immediately before 08.04, and Summarize
Written Text facts were reverified before 08.05. Each remaining task step must
recheck its current official rubric before implementation.
Authored key points, illustrative summaries and local coverage/fluency diagnostics
cannot reproduce Pearson's scoring. The app must label its results as practice
metrics. No official overall score, cloud scoring dependency or human accuracy
claim follows from these contracts.
